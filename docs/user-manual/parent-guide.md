# SafeRide Parent Guide

This guide is for parents and guardians. SafeRide lets you follow your child's school bus, get a notification the moment your child boards, approaches home, or is dropped off, and cancel a ride on days your child isn't travelling.

## Getting access

Your school registers your child in SafeRide and records your **name, phone number and email address** on the child's profile. That email address is your key to the app.

**To create your account:**

1. Open `https://saferidelive.co.ke/auth` in your phone's browser.
2. Tap **Sign up** at the bottom of the card.
3. Fill in your **full name**, your **email — it must be exactly the email you gave the school** — and a password (at least 6 characters). Under **"I am a"**, choose **Parent**.
4. Submit. You are signed in immediately, and every child whose record carries your email is linked to your account automatically.

If the Home screen says **"No children are linked to your account yet."**, the email you signed up with does not match what the school has on file. Contact the school office to add or correct your email on the child's record.

Up to **two parents** can have accounts linked to each child — the school records a "Parent 1" and an optional "Parent 2". Both receive the same notifications.

**Signing in later:** use the **Email & Password** tab (ignore the **Driver PIN** tab — that is for drivers). If you forget your password, tap **"Forgot password?"** and follow the emailed link. Reset links work **once** and expire quickly, so use the newest email if you request more than one.

You stay signed in on your phone until you sign out. On a **shared or borrowed phone, always sign out** (Profile → **Sign Out**) — this also stops that device from receiving your family's notifications.

## The app at a glance

Four tabs sit at the bottom of the screen:

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | One card per child: current status, bus, driver, quick actions |
| **Track** | The live route map and stop-by-stop progress |
| **Alerts** | Every notification about your children's buses, last 7 days |
| **Profile** | Your details, your children, the notifications switch, sign out |

The screens refresh themselves every few seconds — no need to pull or reload.

## Home — today at a glance

Each child's card shows their name, grade and bus, plus a live status badge:

- **At home** — before pickup, or after being dropped off at home
- **On bus** — the driver confirmed your child boarded
- **Expected on bus** — the afternoon bus has left school and your child is on its list, but the driver has not confirmed them yet. It means "we expect so", not "we checked"
- **At school** — safely at school
- **Dropped off** — drop-off confirmed at their stop, or the driver recorded that your child left the bus elsewhere (you will get a note saying where)
- **Absent today** — marked absent for today
- **Being confirmed** — the school does not yet know where your child is, and someone will call you. You do not need to do anything until they do; if you already know where your child is, ring the office and it saves them a call

These are the exact words the app uses. If you ever see something that looks like a code — `on-bus`, `dropped-off` — tell the office; that is a fault, not a status.

While your child is on the bus, the card also shows an **approximate arrival estimate** ("Arriving in ~N min"). Treat it as a rough guide only — the reliable signals are the stop-by-stop progress on the **Track** tab and the **"Bus approaching"** notification, which fires when the bus reaches the stop just before yours.

Two quick actions sit under the cards: **Alerts** (opens the Alerts tab) and **Call Driver**, which dials the driver's phone directly. Call Driver is greyed out if no driver phone is on file yet.

## Track — following the bus

Open **Track** and, if you have more than one child, pick the child from the selector at the top.

- The **map** shows the route's stops joined by a green line. Stops turn **green as the bus completes them**; upcoming stops stay grey.
- While the bus is transmitting, a pulsing **"{Bus} is live"** badge appears under the map. The map shows progress stop by stop rather than a continuously moving bus icon.
- Below the map, the **stop list** shows every stop in order, ticking off completed ones. Your child's stop carries a green **"Your stop"** badge; the school is marked **"School"**.

**Privacy:** you see your own child's name and your own stop's full address, but other families' stops are anonymised (house numbers removed) and other children's names are never shown. Other parents see your stop the same way.

## Notifications

SafeRide records an alert for every meaningful moment of your child's trip:

| Notification | When it arrives |
| --- | --- |
| **Bus on the way** | The morning pickup run has started — get your child ready |
| **Boarded the bus** | Your child got on the bus |
| **Bus approaching** | The bus reached the stop just before yours |
| **Arrived at school** | Your child reached school safely |
| **On the way home** | The afternoon run home has started |
| **Dropped off** | Your child left the bus. Usually at their own stop; if it was somewhere else, the message says where and who met them |
| **Marked absent** | The driver marked your child absent. The message says **which trip** — the morning pickup or the ride home — and says nothing about the other one |
| **Correction** | The driver corrected something they had recorded by mistake. The earlier message no longer applies; this one does |
| **Incident** | The driver reported a breakdown, accident or delay on the bus |
| **School notice** | The school office sent a message to your route |
| **Ride cancelled** | A parent on the account cancelled a ride (both parents are told) |

All of these always appear in the **Alerts** tab. Push notifications — the ones that pop up on your phone even when the app is closed — are an optional extra layer on top.

**One deliberate silence.** If the school ends up not knowing where your child is, you get **no automated message** — your child's card reads **"Being confirmed"** and a person from the office phones you. That is on purpose: a push notification is the wrong way to hear that news, and the office would rather tell you themselves.

**And one thing the app will never do:** claim your child arrived somewhere nobody confirmed. If the driver did not record your child boarding, you will not get an "Arrived at school" message about them — the absence of a message is not the same as nothing having happened, so if you are unsure, call the office.

### Turning on push notifications

1. Go to **Profile** and tap **"Enable Push Notifications"**.
2. When your browser asks for permission, tap **Allow**.

**On iPhone/iPad** push only works if you first install the app to your home screen: in Safari tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**, then open SafeRide from the new icon and enable notifications from there (requires iOS 16.4 or newer). On Android and on computers, enabling it in the browser is enough.

If the button reads **"Notifications blocked in browser"**, you previously declined permission — re-allow notifications for the site in your browser settings, then try again. If it reads **"Push not supported on this device"** or **"Push not configured on this server"**, push isn't available, but you lose nothing important: the **Alerts tab still shows everything**, refreshed every few seconds while the app is open.

Tapping a push notification opens the Alerts tab.

### The Alerts tab

- **Recent** shows the last 24 hours; **History** shows the 6 days before that.
- Filter by period (**All / Morning / Afternoon**) or by type.
- Unread alerts are highlighted; opening the tab marks them read. Alerts can't be deleted — they age out of History after 7 days.

## Cancelling a ride

If your child won't take the bus today, cancel the ride so the driver doesn't wait at your stop:

1. On **Home**, tap **"Cancel ride"** on the child's card.
2. Choose **Morning**, **Afternoon**, or **Rest of day**, then confirm.

The school office and the other linked parent are notified, and the driver's route skips your stop. The child's card shows a chip such as **"AM ride cancelled"** with a **"Withdraw"** link — tap **Withdraw** to restore the ride if plans change.

A few rules apply, and the app explains each one when it stops you:

- You can't cancel a run that has **already been completed**, or one your child is **on the bus for right now** — call the office or the driver instead.
- If the **school has already marked your child absent**, only the office can change it.
- Once a run has started, that half of the day can no longer be withdrawn.
- Cancellations apply to **today only**. For a longer absence, tell the school office.

## Profile

The Profile tab shows your name, email and phone, and a card for each linked child. These details are **managed by the school** — if anything is wrong (name, phone, address, bus), ask the office to update it. The only controls here are the push notifications switch and **Sign Out**.

## Quick fixes

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "No children are linked to your account yet." | Your sign-up email doesn't match the school's records. Ask the office to correct the email on the child's profile. |
| No push notifications arriving | Check Profile → the button should read "Disable Push Notifications" (meaning push is on). On iPhone, make sure you opened the app from the home-screen icon. Check the phone's notification settings for your browser. |
| Password reset link says "Reset link invalid" | Links are single-use and expire quickly. Tap "Request a new reset link" and use the newest email. |
| The arrival estimate seems off | It's approximate. Rely on the Track tab's stop progress and the "Bus approaching" alert. |
| "Too many cancellation changes." | There's an hourly limit on cancelling/withdrawing. Wait a while, or call the office. |
| Wrong phone, address or stop shown | Contact the school office — parents can't edit these in the app. |
